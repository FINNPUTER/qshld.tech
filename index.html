How a wallet gets drained after Q-Day
01

You send a transaction.

To sign it, your wallet publishes your public key. Until that moment the chain only knew a hash of it, and a hash cannot be reversed.

tx.sign(privateKey) reveals publicKey
02

The key stays public forever.

Blockchains never forget. Every key revealed since 2009 sits in plain sight, indexed and searchable by anyone who runs a node.

exposed keys on ledger: all of them
03

Harvest now, decrypt later.

Nobody has to hack anything today. The data is already public. An attacker only needs to wait for the hardware to arrive.

wait(until: faultTolerantQC)
04

Shor's algorithm runs.

A fault tolerant quantum computer solves the elliptic curve problem behind ECDSA and Ed25519. Public key goes in, private key comes out.

shor(publicKey) returns privateKey
05

Move before it happens.

Funds on an address that never signed anything still hide behind a hash. On Solana the address is the public key, so every SOL wallet is exposed by design.
